Charlesbourg's housing stock

Charlesbourg is an established residential neighbourhood with a wide variety of property types. You'll find plenty of bungalows built between the 1960s and 1980s, split-level homes, two- and three-unit plexes, as well as condos in more recent developments.

Each building type has its own characteristics: bungalows often have basements to finish or refresh, plexes require coordination between units, and condos come with co-ownership rules to respect. We understand these realities and adapt our approach accordingly. The on-site estimate is the chance to go through everything together — no pressure, no obligation.
