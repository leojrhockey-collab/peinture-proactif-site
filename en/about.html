Who we are

More than 10 years of painting
in the Québec City region.

Peinture Proactif is a Québec City painting company serving homeowners and businesses throughout the region. We've built our reputation on honest estimates, careful work and spaces left impeccably clean.

Whether it's a single room or an entire building, we bring the same level of attention to every project. No shortcuts, no surprises — just quality work delivered on time.

Our values

What drives us

Four principles that guide every project we take on.

Honesty

Our estimates are clear and complete before we start. What we quote is what you pay — no hidden extras.

Attention to detail

Every surface is prepared properly. We never rush the prep to get to the painting faster — the quality of a finish depends on it.

Respect

We treat every space as if it were our own — furniture protected, floors covered, and everything left cleaner than we found it.

Reliability

We show up when we say we will, finish when we planned to, and communicate throughout — so you're never left wondering.
